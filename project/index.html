How to Use PEC EnviroView

A quick overview of the tools available in this map.

Layers
Toggle map layers on or off in the Layers panel. Open the sidebar with the ☰ button in the top-left corner.
Upload
Add your own KMZ, KML, GeoJSON, or JSON files using the Upload panel in the sidebar. Uploads persist through page reloads.
Measure
Select Distance or Area in the Measure panel, then click points on the map. Click Clear to reset the measurement.
Collect
Open the Collect panel and press Start Collecting, then tap the map to place labeled points. Fill in the form and save. Export all points as GeoJSON.
Legend
The legend in the bottom-left corner updates automatically as layers are toggled on or off. Use the eye icon to hide it.
3D Terrain
Press the terrain button (mountain icon) in the top-right controls to enable 3D elevation. Right-click and drag, or use two fingers, to tilt the view.
Geolocate
Press the crosshair icon in the top-right controls to center the map on your current location. Enable location access when prompted.
